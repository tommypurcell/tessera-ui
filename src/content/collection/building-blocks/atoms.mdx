Every named primitive in this source is intentionally small: it owns one visual atom or native control and leaves larger composition to the consuming component.

The shared contract is deliberately predictable: `xs` through `xl` sizes map to 24, 32, 40, 48, and 56px; semantic tones stay consistent across signals, controls, and progress; focus-visible rings remain outside the control; native inputs retain keyboard behavior; and motion is guarded with `motion-safe` utilities. Dark classes are included at the atom boundary so composed components do not need to rewrite every state.

These are the pieces to compose into buttons, cards, dialogs, menus, tables, and sidebars—not finished components by themselves.
